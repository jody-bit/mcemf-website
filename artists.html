Andrea Kalajian

Andrea Kalajian is an auditory storyteller whose sets take listeners on an intimate journey, blending soft melodies with harder pushes to build energy. She crafts every set to fit the vibe of the moment, factoring in location and time of day.

InstagramSoundCloudLinktree

Brent Shay

Brent Shay is a Detroit-based DJ deeply rooted in the city's techno and house scene. After relocating to Flint in his early twenties, he returned in 2018 with a focus on dark, banging techno with industrial and acid elements. He's a resident DJ at Fnoob Techno Radio (London) and Kapital Techno Radio (Berlin).

Instagram

case managerz

case managerz is a Detroit-area duo made up of Ryan (born in Clinton Township) and Matthew (born in Roseville), united by a shared love of English New Wave, Daft Punk, Depeche Mode, and the entire Basic Channel catalog. That record-collector's ear shows up in every set they play.

Instagram

Cody Hammer

Cody Hammer is a resident DJ at Minimal Detroit with a career spanning two decades. He regularly plays and helps manage Minimal Detroit's monthly residency series SPKR PEOPLE, as well as their annual Memorial Day party Breakfast of Champions at SPKRBOX, creating space for fellow artists while showcasing his own deep track selection. With production partner Lock & Hammer, he's released music on Techno House Connoisseurs and Party Store Records, including a remix by Population One.

InstagramSoundCloudBandcamp

Dedicated Spuddler

Dedicated Spuddler (Marcus Gabriel) is a longtime electronic, electroacoustic, and avant-garde musician with a Dada-influenced streak and a love of intonarumori. He performs weekly at the IT Cabaret in Madison Heights on Sunday nights.

YouTubeInstagram

Dilemma

Dilemma (Derek Chase) is a Detroit DJ and producer who has been active in the city's house and techno scene for nearly two decades. A regular at iconic Detroit venues like TV Lounge, he frequently shares bills with Detroit underground mainstays such as DJ Godfather and Will Webb. He's also associated with local curation projects like the Sunday Dinner Playlist.

InstagramFacebook

DJ Disc Detroit

Dj Disc...Detroit is a veteran underground vinyl DJ with more than 20 years in the game. His funky techno sets have made him one of Detroit's most in-demand artists, and he mentors up-and-coming DJs from around the city.

InstagramFacebook

Dokk Hollidae

Dokk Hollidae brings trap and bass music straight from Chesterfield, MI, with heavy, emotionally-charged drops, glitchy, boundary-pushing sound design, and a dark low end built to hit hard. Influenced by Alison Wonderland, Skrillex, and Nitepunk, his sets are engineered to move — high-energy, bass-forward, and built to get the crowd's heart pumping.

InstagramSoundCloud

Duotron

Duotron are Detroit-area electronic musicians crafting ambient, drone, and beat-driven music, with over 20 years performing and recording together. Their sound draws on the glitch and texture experimentation pioneered by artists like Oval and Mouse on Mars, layered with the atmospheric tradition of ambient pioneers Brian Eno and Hans-Joachim Roedelius. Expect sets that lean as much into warped, hypnotic soundscapes as into rhythm.

ILLogical

ILLogical (Greg) is a bass music producer and DJ out of Clarkston, MI, and one half of the bass duo Locally Grown on Detroit's Motor City Bass label. He's shared stages with acts like Black Carl & Copycatt, Crawdad Sniper & KLO, Bukez Finezt, and Tripp St, at venues ranging from The Magic Stick to Electric Forest's renegade stages. Expect loud, original, sound-system-driven noise built to leave the crowd smiling and refreshed.

JH-X9

JH-X9 (Justin Horbes) is Metro Detroit's self-described mad synth-pop scientist, based out of Clinton Township. He released his first album as a teenager back in 2016 and has been building on that sound ever since, drawing on synth-pop and EBM touchstones like New Order, Front 242, Inner City, and Model 500. Expect a keytar-driven live show — Horbes brings the riffs and the stage presence in equal measure.

Neil V.

Neil V. is a Detroit techno DJ and producer who got his start in the city's underground warehouse scene as a teenager. A member of Detroit Techno Militia since the early 2000s, he's known for a precise, versatile blending style built over thirty-plus years.

InstagramMixcloud

Scott Ashley

Scott Ashley is a Detroit-based DJ, producer, and engineer whose sets move through jazz, funk, soul, house, techno, and ambient sounds, shaped by growing up on Detroit radio staples like the Electrifying Mojo, progressive post-disco, and WJZZ. He's played venues across the city — including MotorCity Wine, The Gold Bar, SpkrBox, TV Lounge, and Woodbridge Pub — and produces and performs live electronic music under the moniker DeepSEA, including work on the AEtherwaves compilation series. Expect an eclectic, futuristic mix rooted in funk, soul, and jazz.

Tangle Garden

Tangle Garden is an emerging independent DJ and artist from Detroit, known for a distinctive blend of techno and dub techno. Raised on a foundation of experimental sound, they bring a sharp ear for texture, rhythm, and theatricality to every set, drawing on the atmospheric, story-driven house and ambient influences that artists like Leon Vynehall are known for.
